# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.

The full text is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project
maintainers at **info@fiavaion.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
